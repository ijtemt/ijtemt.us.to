CALL FOR PAPERS

LAST DATE FOR PAPER SUBMISSION: 25th OF EVERY MONTH

IJTEMT invites original research papers, review papers and scholarly articles for publication in the fields of Economics, Management and Technology. Contributions from research scholars, academicians, professionals and industry practitioners are welcome.

All submitted papers should be original and previously unpublished. A manuscript should not be under simultaneous consideration by another journal. Articles are subject to editorial screening and peer review.

  • Submission deadline: 25th of every month
  • Publication fee: USD 10 for online publication
  • Submission email: ijtemt@gmail.com
  • Publication frequency: 6 issues per year (bi-monthly)
