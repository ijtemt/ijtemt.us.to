Guidelines for Paper Submission

Guidelines for Submission and Review Process

IJTEMT follows an editorial screening and peer-review process. Manuscripts are first checked for scope, basic quality and originality. Suitable manuscripts are then forwarded for double-blind peer review.

a. Initial editorial screening and plagiarism/originality check.
b. Suitable manuscripts are forwarded for double-blind peer review.
c. Reviewers may recommend acceptance, acceptance subject to revision, resubmission after major revision, or rejection.
d. Revised manuscripts may be sent to the same reviewers for further evaluation.
e. The Editorial Board reserves the right to decline manuscripts that do not meet the journal's scope, quality or ethical requirements.

Submission of Manuscript

Authors should submit the manuscript in .doc/.docx and PDF formats to ijtemt@gmail.com. The manuscript should contain all relevant figures, tables and appendices.

By submitting a manuscript, the authors confirm that the work is original, has not been published previously and is not under consideration by another journal.

Publication Fee

Publication fee: USD 10 per accepted paper for online publication.

Payment instructions, where applicable, will be communicated by the Editorial Office after acceptance. Authors should not send payment unless instructed by the journal.

Open Access License Policy

IJTEMT provides online access to published articles for readers. Authors should review the journal's copyright and publishing agreement before final publication. The applicable rights and reuse conditions are those stated in the current IJTEMT copyright agreement.
